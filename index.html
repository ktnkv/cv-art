I build visuals and light in TouchDesigner. In my work I use feedback loops, cellular automata, fractals and the like. I’m drawn to systems that produce complex, unusual behavior while staying, as far as possible, simple at their core.

Work

Light, image and sound work together here — best watched with sound on.

cells

A cellular automaton spread across the wall. Every patch lives only briefly: it grows, breaks into lace-like edges and dissolves, making room for the next.

grid

A web left by a creature no one has seen — and it may not have gone far. The threads tighten and slacken on their own, close enough to touch, close enough to take you.

hypno

Made for a techno rave. On every bar the feedback loop resets and the light steps out.

series

infossae

A series built around a single sign — a crawling spider crossed with the biohazard symbol. In every piece it stays the same and serves as the seed — the rest grows out of it.
